#### Current Focus

**[Revlet Platform](https://github.com/codexshivam/revlet)** · [revlet.me](https://revlet.me) · [app.revlet.me](https://app.revlet.me) · [mail.revlet.me](https://mail.revlet.me)

Revlet Platform is an enterprise cloud infrastructure control plane designed to replace fragmented IT, DNS, and email tooling with a single sovereign architecture. It unifies isolated multi-organization tenants, granular RBAC, FIDO2 passkey step-up security, authoritative Cloudflare DNS management with automatic pre-change snapshot rollbacks, and custom-domain business email through **RevletMail**—powered by Stalwart Mail Server (JMAP), Cloudflare R2, and AWS SES with enforced MTA-STS, shared mailboxes, outbound DLP, Legal Holds, and SIEM audit logging.

**[NidanIQ](https://github.com/codexshivam/nidaniq)**

NidanIQ is a vector-borne disease surveillance platform engineered for the Epidemiology and Disease Control Division (EDCD) under the Ministry of Health and Population, Government of Nepal. Built to support national public health operations, it streamlines case reporting, epidemiological tracking, and early outbreak detection across regional health networks so decision-makers can respond faster with reliable field data.
